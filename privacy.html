Privacy

Last updated October 12, 2026.

Busifier is a managed service operated by Aaron Ray Barlow, doing business as Busifier. In this page, “we” means our team.

What we collect

  • Owner contact information you give us (name, email, phone, business details).
  • Content and account details from sources you authorize, within the permissions available on each platform.
  • Site content you approve for your website.
  • Messages and voice interactions you choose to use for website planning or edits.

How we use it

We use your information to build and maintain your website and local search presence, and to run the service. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for ad targeting.

Site analytics, cookies and your choices

The public pages of busifier.com use two analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site: Microsoft Clarity and the Cloudflare page counter (Cloudflare Web Analytics). Clarity records page views, clicks, scrolling and anonymised session replays. Text you type into forms and other sensitive text is masked before it reaches Clarity, and we do not use the data for advertising. Session data is kept by Microsoft for up to 30 days. The owner app (after sign-in) does not load Clarity.

This is everything the public pages store in your browser today:

  • Clarity, first-party cookies on busifier.com: _clck (a random visitor id, up to 1 year) and _clsk (links the pages of one visit, 1 day). Clarity also keeps _cltk in local storage.
  • Clarity and Bing, set by Microsoft on clarity.ms and bing.com: MUID (about 13 months), CLID (1 year), SM, MR (7 days), SRM_B and ANONCHK. See the Clarity cookie list.
  • Cloudflare page counter: a small script that counts page loads. It sets no cookies.
  • Our own local storage: bz-intro and bz-intro-at (remember you saw the home page intro), bz-beta-dismissed (remembers you closed the product update) and bz-analytics-off (remembers your privacy choice). None of these leave your browser.

How to opt out. Use the “Your privacy choices” link in the footer of any page, or , and turn analytics off. We then stop loading Clarity and the Cloudflare page counter on that browser and clear the Clarity cookies we can reach. We also turn analytics off automatically when your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, with nothing to click. You can also opt out of Microsoft analytics for every site through the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.

Connected social accounts

You can choose to connect your Facebook Page, Instagram professional account, YouTube channel or TikTok account to Busifier. Connecting is optional, and you start it yourself, on the first setup step or from the Account page of your Busifier dashboard.

Your dashboard shows which social accounts you can connect. You can also paste links to your public pages and provide details directly in the chat without connecting an account.

What we read

  • Facebook and Instagram: the name of the Page you pick and its web address, which we build from the Page’s ID, the Instagram username linked to it, the public business details on that Page (category, description, phone, website, city and opening hours) and the Instagram bio, and your recent public posts, with their captions, photos, links and dates. We do not read or keep a street address.
  • YouTube: your channel name and description, and the titles, descriptions, thumbnails, links and dates of your recent public videos.
  • TikTok: your display name, and the titles, descriptions, cover images, links and dates of your recent videos.

Access is read only. We never post, comment, message, edit or delete anything on your accounts, and we do not read private videos or private messages.

Why we read it

We use it only to build and update your Busifier website: to suggest facts and wording from your business details and captions, to offer your Facebook and Instagram photos for your site, and to offer new posts as website updates. YouTube and TikTok videos appear on your site as links or embedded players; we do not copy them. Nothing from your accounts goes on your website until you approve it. We do not sell this information, share it for advertising, or use it to target ads.

Where it is stored

The access token that lets us read your account is encrypted before it is saved, and it is used only by our service to fetch your posts. The posts we read and any photos you choose to use are stored in your Busifier account in Microsoft Azure. We keep the most recent posts we read until you disconnect or ask us to delete them. For YouTube, we delete saved channel details and videos once they are more than 30 days old, unless we have read them again from YouTube in that time.

How to disconnect and delete

  • Press Disconnect next to the account on the Account page of your dashboard. This deletes the saved access token and the posts and business details we saved from that account. For Facebook we also ask Meta to cancel our access. For Instagram we delete our copy, but Instagram keeps the connection until you remove Busifier under Settings, Apps and websites in your Instagram account. A photo or business detail you already approved for your website stays on your website until you ask us to change or remove it.
  • You can also remove Busifier in your Facebook or Google account settings. For Facebook, that triggers our Meta data deletion callback, which deletes the token and the saved posts and business details for your account; you can check a request at the status link Meta shows you. Removing Busifier in Instagram does not notify us yet, so email hello@busifier.com and we will delete what we hold.
  • To ask for deletion in any other way, email hello@busifier.com.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. YouTube features use the YouTube API Services, and by connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can also review and remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions (also reachable at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).

AI processing

The AI Builder, site design, the AI chat editor and voice features use AI models hosted in Microsoft Azure AI Foundry (OpenAI models) and Azure speech services. What you tell the AI Builder, the business details we read from accounts you connect (such as your Page or channel name, category, description, phone, hours and city) so the assistant can ask you to confirm them, the content you approve, and your voice when you use voice are sent to these services to write and edit your website and to reply to you. We do not sell this information, and we do not use information from your connected accounts to train AI models.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while you are a customer. You can ask us to delete it at any time and we will, except for records we need to keep for billing or by law.

Subprocessors

These companies process information for us to run Busifier today:

  • Microsoft Azure: hosting and storage, the servers and containers that run and build the service, Entra External ID for sign-in, Communication Services for the emails we send, and Azure AI Foundry and speech services for AI and voice (see AI processing above).
  • Stripe: payments, subscriptions and sales tax calculation. Stripe collects your card details directly.
  • Cloudflare: DNS for busifier.com.
  • Microsoft Clarity: analytics on the public pages of busifier.com, with text masked (see Site analytics above).
  • Microsoft Azure App Service Domains and its registrar partner (GoDaddy): domain registration, renewal and transfer, only when Busifier registers a domain for you.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google (YouTube) and TikTok: only if you choose to connect an account (see Connected social accounts above).

We update this list when it changes.
