What can shape your website

  • Before-and-after photos of paint correction, ceramic coating and interior rescues.
  • Short clips of a foam bath, a clay bar pass or a finished car rolling out.
  • Package announcements such as a new ceramic coating tier.
  • Schedule notes: closed this Saturday, booked solid until the 14th.

Share these details with the AI Builder, and add your own photos if you like. You decide what belongs on your website.

What gets updated on your website

Each finished-car photo set can become a gallery entry for the right service. Tell the AI chat editor about a new package and it drafts a change to your services list. A price you mention is held as a fact change, so the price on your website only changes after you approve it.

Nothing goes live without your approval. You can also type a change to the AI chat editor in plain words.

Local search for auto detailing

  • Detailing is searched by service and town, such as ceramic coating near me or interior detailing in your city. Your site should name the services you offer and the towns you serve in plain text.
  • Mobile detailers often have no shop to visit. Google lets service-area businesses hide the street address and list the areas served instead; your approved site facts are ready to mirror on your Business Profile.
  • Fresh photos on a Business Profile and on the site show that the business is active. Seasonal specials, such as a pre-winter sealant, can be posted in both places.
  • Hours matter: customers often check weekend and by-appointment hours before they call.

Listing support for your Google Business Profile is separate from your website and depends on eligibility and account access; ask us what fits your business. We cannot promise rankings.

Auto detailing questions

Can Busifier show my before-and-after photos?

Use images you provide and have permission to publish. You approve the website before it goes live.

What if I am a mobile detailer with no shop?

Your site can show your service area without publishing your home address. Google Business Profile support depends on eligibility and authorized account access.

Will my ceramic coating prices show on my site?

Only if you want them to. A price you give the AI Builder is a fact change that needs your explicit approval before it is published.

How do I tell the site I am closed on a given Saturday?

Type it to the AI chat editor, for example "closed Saturday the 11th". It drafts the change and waits for your approval.
