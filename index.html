When do I pay?

You add a payment method only after you approve your finished website. The first $79, plus applicable sales tax, is charged when the site is live; it renews monthly from that date.

Cancel renewal anytime

Cancelling stops the next renewal. Once your site is live, that month’s charge is not refundable except where required by applicable law; before that point you have paid nothing. This policy does not waive your rights concerning unauthorized or erroneous charges.

Questions owners ask

Do I need to write all the pages myself?

No. Tell the AI Builder what you do and who you serve, and add the photos you have. We use that context to build your website. You review every page before anything goes live. No social account is required.

What if the first version is not right?

Tell us what needs to change before your site goes live. Unlimited changes for normal business use. You approve each change before it goes live.

When do I pay?

You add a payment method only after you have approved your finished website and its web address. The first $79, plus applicable sales tax, is charged when the site is live, and it renews monthly from that date. Cancel renewal anytime from your dashboard.

How do I keep my site current?

Ask the AI chat editor for a change in plain words, review the draft and approve the preview before publication. Nothing is published without your approval.

Who owns my site and content?

You keep ownership of the content you supply. Cancellation stops renewal; it does not require a buyout. If you cancel before 12 months of live service, you keep the content you supplied (text, photos and logo) and receive a copy of your site files, and hosting ends with the service. Optional. Own the generated website code for $250 after 12 months of live service. There is no earlier option. Arranged by email: email hello@busifier.com and we send a checkout link and the files. The buyout covers the website code only: a domain Busifier registers for a customer is always theirs. Once your site is live, that month’s charge is not refundable except where required by applicable law; before that point you have paid nothing. This policy does not waive your rights concerning unauthorized or erroneous charges.

Can customers book or request a quote on my site?

Yes. Every site has a quote and contact form, and each request is sent to your email. If you already use an online booking page, we add a Book button that opens it. Busifier does not run its own booking calendar or take payments on your site.

What doesn't Busifier do?

We don't run paid ads, build online stores, take card payments on your site, run a booking calendar, or post to your social accounts. If you already sell or take bookings online, your site can link to those pages.
