Pricing questions

How much does Busifier cost?

The price is $79 per month plus applicable sales tax, with no setup fee. You pay $0 until your approved site is live. Billing starts after you approve the finished site and it goes live. Create your account to start planning your website.

What is included in the monthly price?

The service includes your website build, hosting, and the AI chat editor for changes you approve. Your first website build is included. Changes are unlimited; you approve each before it goes live. They are included in $79 a month plus applicable sales tax.

How do changes work after launch?

Changes are unlimited; you approve each before it goes live. After your site is live, ask the AI chat editor for a change in plain words. It drafts the change and shows you a preview, and nothing is published until you say yes. There are no per-change charges and no automatic top-ups.

When do I pay?

You add a payment method only after you have approved your finished website and its web address. The first $79, plus applicable sales tax, is charged when the site is live and verified, and it renews monthly from that date. Cancel renewal anytime from your dashboard. Once your site is live, that month’s charge is not refundable except where required by applicable law; before that point you have paid nothing. This policy does not waive your rights concerning unauthorized or erroneous charges.

Are there contracts or hidden fees?

No long-term commitment and no setup fee. You pay $0 until your approved site is live, and you can cancel renewal anytime with no exit fee. Sales tax is added on top where it applies. The optional website-code buyout costs extra only if you choose it.
