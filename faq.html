What is Busifier?

Busifier is a website service for US small businesses. Tell us about your business, review your finished site, then ask for changes in the AI chat editor (free before launch). It is operated by Aaron Ray Barlow, doing business as Busifier.

Do I need to write all the pages myself?

No. Tell the AI Builder what you do and who you serve, and add the photos you have. We use that context to build your website. You review every page before anything goes live. No social account is required.

What if the first version is not right?

Tell us what needs to change before your site goes live. Changes are unlimited; you approve each before it goes live.

Is there a limit on how many changes I can ask for?

Changes are unlimited; you approve each before it goes live. They are included in your $79 a month plus applicable sales tax. Each request is drafted for your approval before anything is published, and there are no extra charges.

Do I have to buy the website when I cancel?

No. Cancellation stops renewal without a buyout charge. If you cancel before 12 months of live service, you keep the content you supplied (text, photos and logo) and receive a copy of your site files, and hosting ends with the service. Optional. Own the generated website code for $250 after 12 months of live service. There is no earlier option. Arranged by email: email hello@busifier.com and we send a checkout link and the files. The buyout covers the website code only: a domain Busifier registers for a customer is always theirs.

How much does Busifier cost?

The price is $79 per month plus applicable sales tax, with no setup fee. You pay $0 until your approved site is live. Billing starts after you approve the finished site and it goes live. Create your account to start planning your website.

When do I pay?

You add a payment method only after you have approved your finished website and its web address. The first $79, plus applicable sales tax, is charged when the site is live and verified, and it renews monthly from that date. Cancel renewal anytime from your dashboard. Once your site is live, that month’s charge is not refundable except where required by applicable law; before that point you have paid nothing. This policy does not waive your rights concerning unauthorized or erroneous charges.

Do I need to post on social media?

You can use the AI Builder without social accounts. Every website update waits for your approval: approve, edit or dismiss each one. You can also request AI Builder edits, review the draft and approve the preview before publication.

Does anything go live without my approval?

No. Every website change waits for your approval. Changes to prices, hours, phone number or address need an explicit yes from you before they are applied.

What is the AI chat editor?

The dashboard lets you request edits in plain words, review the draft and approve the preview. Your first full website build is included. Changes are unlimited; you approve each before it goes live. They are included in your $79 a month plus applicable sales tax, with no extra charges and no automatic top-ups.

Who handles my Google Business Profile?

Busifier’s full offering includes support for Google, Bing and Apple listings. Keep managing your Google Business Profile yourself until we confirm listing management is active for your business. Your website gives your listings one set of approved facts to point to.

How long until my site is live?

After you confirm your business details, we build your whole website, about 12 minutes, and email you when it is ready to review. Timing after that depends on your revisions and launch checks. We email you at each step, and nothing goes live until you approve.

Do I have to connect my social accounts?

No, it is your choice. Share your business details and photos directly in chat. Connecting a social account is offered as it becomes available. You approve every fact before it goes on your site.

Will Busifier get me to the top of Google?

No one can promise a ranking, and we do not. We do the groundwork that helps search engines and AI assistants read your business correctly: page titles that name your services and towns, structured data, a sitemap, and a lightweight site checked against a speed budget before launch. When your site goes live we publish its sitemap and notify Bing. For Google, Bing and Apple listing management, contact us to confirm access for your business.

Can customers book or request a quote on my site?

Yes. Every site has a quote and contact form, and each request is sent to your email. If you already use an online booking page, we add a Book button that opens it. Busifier does not run its own booking calendar or take payments on your site.

What doesn't Busifier do?

We don't run paid ads, build online stores, take card payments on your site, run a booking calendar, or post to your social accounts. If you already sell or take bookings online, your site can link to those pages.

Are the businesses on your site real customers?

No. The demo businesses shown on this site are fictional examples used to show how the product works.

Where is Busifier based?

Busifier is operated by Aaron Ray Barlow, doing business as Busifier in Midlothian, Virginia, and serves small businesses in the United States.
